`Track shifts, fuel & earnings. Built for ride-hail drivers. Works offline.`

**Full description:**

```
DriverLog is a simple log book for drivers who get paid per trip.

BUILT FOR THE ROAD
• Start a shift, log every drop-off as you go, end the shift — your session is saved automatically.
• Works offline in tunnels and dead zones; everything syncs to your account when you're back online.
• Log in with email or LINE — one account keeps your records safe across phone changes.

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
• Daily, weekly and monthly revenue, net profit and distance at a glance.
• Revenue per trip, fuel cost per kilometer, break-even insights.
• Export everything to CSV or back up your full logbook as a file.

MAINTENANCE THAT PAYS FOR ITSELF
• Track oil changes, tires and service costs against real odometer readings.
• See upcoming service dates before they become breakdowns.

FOR FLEET OWNERS
• Create a fleet, invite your drivers, and see aggregated revenue, trips and efficiency across everyone — only from drivers who accepted your invite.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your logbook belongs to you. Fleet owners only ever see data from drivers who explicitly joined their fleet.
• Full privacy policy: https://driverlog.link/privacy

Free to use. Thai language supported (ไทย).
```

```

• เริ่มกะ เก็บทริปทุกจุดส่งผู้โดยสารระหว่างทาง ปิดกะแล้วระบบบันทึกให้อัตโนมัติ

• ล็อกอินด้วยอีเมลหรือ LINE บัญชีเดียวกันใช้ได้ทุกเครื่อง ข้อมูลไม่หายแม้เปลี่ยนมือถือ
```
